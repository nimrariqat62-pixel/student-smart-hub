TV Remote Control Teardown & Circuit Analysis (Nikki)

Overview: Physical teardown of a standard TV remote to examine internal circuit boards, microchips, and signal transmitters.

External View

Internal PCB View

  • Green PCB Board: The main circuit board with etched copper pathways that route electrical signals.
  • Microcontroller IC: The central microchip (brain) that processes which button was pressed.
  • IR LED & Solder Joints: The infrared diode at the top that beams wireless signals directly to the television.
  • Carbon Contact Pads: Rubber keypad layers with conductive carbon grids that complete the circuit loops.
