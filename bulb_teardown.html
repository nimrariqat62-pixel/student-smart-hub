Practical Teardown Overview

  • Diffuser Dome: White plastic cover jo LED ki roshni ko barabar phailata hai.
  • SMD LED PCB: Gol circuit plate jis par series mein choti LED chips lagi hoti hain.
  • Driver Components: Capacitors aur resistors jo AC voltage ko LED ke liye regulate karte hain.
  • Base & Housing: Screw base (holder connection) aur body jahan external damage spot check kiya gaya.
